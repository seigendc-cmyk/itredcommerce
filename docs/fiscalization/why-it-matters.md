# Why Fiscalisation Matters for Growth

- Unlocks B2B revenue: VAT-registered buyers can only claim input tax on
  fiscalised invoices — an unfiscalised supplier is invisible to that segment.
- Builds credible financial history: fiscalised sales records are the kind
  of verifiable revenue evidence banks/microfinance lenders want, and they
  compound with iTred's own BI Brain data (COGS, revenue) into a stronger
  loan case.
- Reduces audit risk: real-time reporting means no year-end reconciliation
  surprises.
- Competitive leveler: once mandatory, informal competitors undercutting via
  tax evasion lose that advantage the moment enforcement catches up.
- Regional norm, not a Kenya-specific quirk: Zambia, Malawi, and Mozambique
  are following Kenya's eTIMS model — this is infrastructure the region is
  converging on regardless.
